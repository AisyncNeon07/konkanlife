Temporal Guide

Seasonal Rhythms of Konkan

From festive springtime rituals to emerald monsoons and tranquil winter tides.

March — May

Mango Harvest & Shimgo

Experience the legendary Ratnagiri Alphonso season, vibrant Shimgo (Konkan Holi) folk rituals, and Olive Ridley turtle hatchling releases along Velas beach.

28°C - 34°C • Golden Skies
June — September

The Emerald Monsoon

The Western Ghats transform into misty cloud forests. Cascading waterfalls emerge across Amboli and Savdav, while sea fort walls glisten in lush moss.

Lush Greenery • Dramatic Rains
October — February

Tranquil Winter Seas

The golden era for ocean exploration. Calm turquoise waters, crisp coastal breezes, scuba diving at Malvan, and comfortable trekking conditions.
